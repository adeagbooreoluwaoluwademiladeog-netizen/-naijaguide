Online Safety

7 Warning Signs of a Fake Online Giveaway

Online giveaways can be genuine, but some are designed to trick people into sharing personal information or sending money.

  1. They ask for money first. A request for a fee before receiving a supposed prize is a major warning sign.
  2. They demand sensitive information. Be cautious when a giveaway asks for passwords, PINs or unnecessary financial details.
  3. They create extreme urgency. Messages such as “pay within 10 minutes” are designed to stop you from thinking carefully.
  4. The account or page looks suspicious. Check its history, spelling, contact details and official links.
  5. The prize sounds unrealistic. An extraordinary reward with no clear rules deserves careful checking.
  6. You are told to forward the message to everyone. Viral sharing does not prove that an offer is legitimate.
  7. The link looks strange. Check the destination before entering any information.

Bottom line: Stop and verify independently before paying, sharing sensitive information or following a suspicious link.

Technology

How to Protect Your WhatsApp Account

Protecting an account starts with strong security habits.

  • Turn on two-step verification in WhatsApp settings.
  • Never share your WhatsApp verification code with another person.
  • Review linked devices regularly and remove devices you do not recognise.
  • Use a screen lock on your phone.
  • Keep WhatsApp and your phone's operating system updated.
  • Be careful with unexpected links and messages asking for urgent action.

How to Create a Good CV as a Student

A student CV should be clear, truthful and easy to scan.

Include

  • Your name and appropriate contact details.
  • A short personal or career profile.
  • Education and relevant courses.
  • Useful skills, such as communication, computer skills or teamwork.
  • Relevant volunteering, projects, leadership or work experience.
  • Achievements that are genuine and relevant.

Keep the layout clean and check spelling before sending it.

Online Safety

7 Signs an Online Job Offer May Be Fake

  1. You are asked to pay a fee before getting the job.
  2. The recruiter refuses to provide verifiable company information.
  3. The pay is unusually high for very little work.
  4. You are pressured to act immediately.
  5. The communication comes from an unrelated or suspicious address.
  6. You are asked for unnecessary sensitive information early in the process.
  7. The job description is vague and the recruitment process makes no sense.

Research the company independently and use official contact channels before trusting an offer.

Digital Skills

Beginner's Guide to Digital Marketing

Digital marketing is the use of online channels to reach and help a target audience.

Core areas

  • Content: create useful information for a specific audience.
  • Social media: build conversations and awareness on relevant platforms.
  • Search: make useful pages easier to discover through search engines.
  • Email: communicate with people who have chosen to hear from you.
  • Analytics: measure what works and improve it.

Start with one audience, one clear goal and useful content. Consistency and measurement matter more than trying everything at once.

Simple Ways to Make Your Phone More Secure

  • Use a strong passcode or device authentication.
  • Install system and app updates.
  • Only install apps from trusted sources.
  • Review app permissions and remove unnecessary access.
  • Use screen lock and device-finding features.
  • Back up important information.
  • Avoid entering sensitive information on suspicious websites.
